Privacy Policy for ArrowIQ

Created and managed by Mathura Digital  |  Website: mathuradigital.com  |  Email: info@mathuradigital.com

Effective date: September 26, 2026  |  Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mathura Digital ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use ArrowIQ (the "App"), an arrow-puzzle and cognitive-quiz mobile game. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

ArrowIQ is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. See "Children's Privacy" below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

You can use ArrowIQ in one of two ways:

  • Guest play — you play without creating an account. We create an anonymous identifier for your session so your local progress works, but we do not collect your name, email, or any other personal information. Guest progress is stored only on your device and is lost if you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Sign in with Google — if you choose to sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account (via Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In) so we can save your progress and let you sign in again on other devices.

The App's code also supports signing in with a phone number, which would collect your phone number via SMS verification. This option is not currently enabled for players.

1.2 Gameplay & Progress Data

Whether you play as a guest or signed in, we process:

  • Level progress and difficulty selections
  • In-game currency and stats: coins, XP, streak count, hearts, achievements
  • Quiz answers and quiz performance statistics
  • App settings: sound, theme, language, and notification preference

If you are signed in with Google, this data is synced to our backend (Supabase) so it's available across devices and used to calculate the daily leaderboard. If you play as a guest, this data stays on your device only.

1.3 Leaderboard

If you are signed in, your display name, daily XP total, and rank may be shown to other players on the in-app daily leaderboard.

1.4 Device & Diagnostic Data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the App encounters an error, including: device model, operating system version, app version, language setting, and crash stack traces. This helps us find and fix bugs. It does not include the contents of your gameplay.

1.5 Advertising Data

ArrowIQ shows ads (e.g. rewarded video ads you can choose to watch for in-game bonuses) using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier and other information to select, deliver, and measure ads, and — unless you've limited ad tracking in your device settings — to personalize the ads you see. See "Advertising & Google AdMob" below for details and how to opt out.

1.6 Notifications

If you allow notifications, ArrowIQ may schedule a local daily reminder to play, using the standard notification permission on your device. This reminder is scheduled and stored entirely on your device — it is not sent from our servers and no data about it is transmitted to us.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the App: save and restore your progress, run the leaderboard, and unlock content
  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure
  • To display ads and measure their performance
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability and performance
  • To communicate with you if you contact us for support
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob

ArrowIQ uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to earn extra coins or a revive). AdMob may use your advertising identifier and other technical information to show ads that are relevant to you, and to measure ad performance and prevent fraud. This may involve sharing information with Google's advertising partners.

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or "Delete advertising ID" on newer Android versions)
  • You can also review and adjust your ad settings directly at adssettings.google.com

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information:

  • With the service providers listed above, solely to operate the App on our behalf
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Mathura Digital, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy continuing to apply to your information

6. Children's Privacy

ArrowIQ is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@mathuradigital.com and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so that your progress remains available. Guest data lives only on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. You can request deletion of your account data at any time — see "Your Rights and Choices" below.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted network connections and secure, access-controlled cloud storage — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Account and Data Deletion; Your Rights and Choices

Request account and data deletion

To request deletion of your ArrowIQ account and associated gameplay data, email us from the email address linked to your Google sign-in. Use the subject ArrowIQ account deletion request.

Request deletion by email

After we verify ownership, we delete the account data associated with your ArrowIQ sign-in, including saved progress, gameplay statistics, leaderboard information, and linked profile details held for the App. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. We may retain limited information only where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Guest progress is stored on your device and can be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

  • Play as a guest to avoid providing any personal information at all
  • Sign out at any time from the App's settings
  • Disable notifications from the App's settings or your device's system settings
  • Limit ad personalization using the steps in "Advertising & Google AdMob" above
  • Access or correct your account data: email info@mathuradigital.com from the address linked to your account.

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection — exercised the same way, by emailing us. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and to opt out of the "sharing" of information for cross-context behavioral advertising (see the ad-personalization opt-out above).

10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Google/Firebase, Supabase, Cloudflare) operate data centers in multiple countries. Your information may be processed in a country other than the one where you live, which may have different data protection laws.
